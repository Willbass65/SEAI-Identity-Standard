## Reddit r/MachineLearning

**Flair:** Research or Discussion

**Title:**
[OC] SEAI Identity Standard — Open-source hardware-rooted identity for AI

**Body:**

ALBOE USA is releasing the SEAI Identity Standard — a hardware-rooted identity framework for autonomous systems.

SEAI introduces:
• Birth Certificates — cryptographic identity documents for every AI agent
• Hardware Attestation — proof that an agent runs on the hardware it claims
• Lineage Tracking — traceable ancestry from origin
• Authority Scopes — permission levels that limit what an agent can do
• Revocation System — kill switch for compromised agents
• Identity Firewall — non-bypassable verification before every action

The goal is simple: give AI a way to earn trust.

SEAI started as an internal idea at ALBOE USA. We applied it to our development work and realized it could help the entire AI ecosystem. So we open-sourced it.

The concept of AI birth certificates originated in our patent work. We created it to solve identity and trust problems in our own development environment. Only later did we realize the idea had global implications.

This is not a product. This is a standard. Free, open, sovereign, and available to the world.
